I specialize in building intelligent systems that integrate data analytics, AI automation, and business logic to transform how organizations operate. How I Got Here

My journey started in engineering, where I graduated from Telecommunications and Electronics Engineering. During my early career, I gained strong hands-on experience in TETRA systems, low current systems, and engineering sales, which helped me develop a solid foundation in analytical thinking, system design, and problem-solving within complex technical environments. Alongside my engineering career, I studied Business Administration for one year, which helped me develop a business mindset and a better understanding of how technical systems connect with organizational and commercial goals. Over time, I transitioned into programming and data analysis, where I discovered the power of data in understanding systems, improving performance, and supporting decision-making processes. This path naturally evolved into AI automation, where I now focus on building intelligent systems that integrate data, automation, and business logic to create scalable and efficient solutions for real-world problems.
