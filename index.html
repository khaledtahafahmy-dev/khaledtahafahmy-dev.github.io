About

Bridging data, AI automation

I am a Data Analyst & AI Automation Engineer specializing in building data-driven systems and intelligent automation workflows that support business decision-making and operational efficiency. I work across data analytics, AI automation, and business systems, applying a structured and analytical approach to solving complex problems and designing scalable solutions.

With a strong foundation in engineering, programming, and business analysis, I bring a systems-thinking mindset that allows me to understand how different components of a system interact and how to optimize them for better performance. My focus is on developing practical and efficient solutions that transform data into actionable insights and automate repetitive or complex business processes using AI and modern tools.

I have experience working with data analysis tools, automation platforms, programming languages, and engineering concepts, which gives me the ability to connect technical implementation with real-world business needs.My goal is to build impactful systems that improve decision-making, enhance efficiency, and support scalable and intelligent business operations without overcomplicating the solution.
